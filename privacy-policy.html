Yargı bölgenize bağlı olarak (örn. GDPR veya KVKK kapsamında), işlenen verilere erişim, düzeltme veya silme talep etme hakkına sahip olabilirsiniz. Uygulama kişisel veri saklamadığından bu haklar esas olarak AdMob'un işlediği veriler için Google'a yöneltilmelidir.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Harf Diyarı" mobile game (the "App") handles your data. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

gameshunterstm
Contact: gameshunterstm@gmail.com

3. Data We Collect / Process

The App does NOT require or collect an account, email address, name, or any other information that directly identifies you. The following data is stored ONLY locally on your device (via SharedPreferences) and is never sent to any server:

  • Your game progress (unlocked level, tracked separately per language)
  • Your lives (life count) and regeneration time
  • Your achievement statistics (levels completed, stars, combos, tiles popped)
  • Your language, sound, and vibration preferences

4. Third-Party Services: Google AdMob

The App uses the Google AdMob SDK for optional rewarded ads (to refill lives), interstitial ads shown between levels, and banner ads shown at the bottom of the game screen. AdMob may process advertising/device identifiers and general device information to serve, measure, and (with your consent) personalize ads. This data is processed by Google, not by the App, under Google's own privacy policy:

  • policies.google.com/privacy
  • google.com/policies/technologies/ads

5. Ad Personalization and Tracking Permission

On iOS devices, the App requests App Tracking Transparency permission in order to show personalized ads. Declining this permission does NOT affect any core functionality of the App (the word game, levels, achievements) in any way — it only results in non-personalized ads being shown instead.

6. Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts

The App does NOT access your location, camera, microphone, or contacts.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience word game suitable for both children and adults, and it does not directly collect personal data. If you plan to publish the App under a children-directed category (e.g. Google Play Families), you must enable "Tag for Child-Directed Treatment" in your AdMob account and separately ensure compliance with applicable regulations (COPPA, GDPR-K, etc.).

8. Data Security and Retention

Local data is protected by your device's own operating system security mechanisms. The App does NOT store your data on any server it operates.

9. Deleting Your Data

Uninstalling the App from your device automatically deletes all locally stored data. You can also manually clear the App's data/cache from your device settings.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. under GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of processed data. Since the App itself does not retain personal data, such requests relating to AdMob's processing should be directed to Google.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available from Settings > Privacy Policy inside the App, and from this page.
